# Content plan (drafts only)

Nothing here goes live without the owner's approval. Output goes to `seo/content/`.

## Research, per main service
1. Queries clients type (questions, "how to", "cost", "documents", "delay", country/city variants,
   comparisons such as "X or Y"). Note intent: informational, comparison, ready to buy.
2. Who ranks now for the main queries (top 3–5): domain, type (government, competitor, blog, forum),
   what they cover, what they miss. Search tools are often not localised: say so and ask the user to
   recheck on the local Google.
3. Official facts with exact source URLs and the date checked. Conflicting or unofficial figures go to
   the "to verify" list, never into the text.

## Brief per service (`brief-<service>.md`)
- 5-line summary, SERP findings table, verified facts table (fact | source | date).
- 5–8 articles forming a cluster around the service page. For each: title (main words first), slug, main query and
  3–5 related, intent, H2/H3 outline, 2–3 sentence intro that answers the question, 3–5 FAQ, internal links,
  sources, priority, language versions worth making.
- "To verify by the expert" list.

## Merged plan (`plan.md`)
Waves: (1) one pillar article per service, (2) the niche angle with least competition, (3) practical
questions, (later) articles blocked by unconfirmed figures. Pace: one article a week or every two weeks.

## Article rules
Required: answers the main question near the top, internal link to the service page and contact, named
author (exact name), official sources for every factual claim. No invented client stories; examples only
if real, anonymised and approved.
Guidelines (see `references/onpage-schema.md`; never a reason on their own to rewrite): title around 60
characters with main words first, description around 120–160, sections with H2s, a FAQ when real questions
exist. Length follows the question: Google has no word-count target, so cover the topic fully and stop.

## CMS helper
If the site has an admin editor, add a live checker (like Yoast): title length, description length,
cover image, word count, H2 count, internal link, named author from the team list, external sources
(excluding social/video links), first-paragraph length, Google preview. Show lengths and counts as
hints, not errors, and label any checker score as an editorial aid, not a search score.
