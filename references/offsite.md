# Authority and local visibility kit

Deliver one file, `seo/visibility-kit.md`, with:

1. **Identity block** to copy everywhere: name, address, phone, email, website, hours, founding year,
   company number, main and extra categories, official social links. Short (≈ 150 chars) and long
   (≈ 700 chars) descriptions written only from `seo/facts.md`.
2. **Where to register**, ranked by impact, each marked Free / Membership / To check:
   Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, national business directories, sector bodies and chambers of
   commerce, the official company profile on LinkedIn, partners and event organisers (ask for a link).
3. **Google Posts**: the first two posts, each based on an existing article, with link and button.
   Rhythm: one a week.
4. **Review requests**: a short message in the site's languages with a `[link]` placeholder for the
   Google review link ("Ask for reviews" in the profile). Rules: ask 3–5 happy clients a month, spread
   over time, reply to every review within 48 h, never offer anything in exchange.
5. **Done / to do** checklist with dates.

One strong link (chamber, partner, press) is worth more than many weak directories.
